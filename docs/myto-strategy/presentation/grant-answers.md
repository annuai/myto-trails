## Q01. Brief of the startup (150 to 300 words)

Myto Moto is the operating system for motorcyclists: one app for trails, rides, group safety, fuel and gear, built in Bengaluru for India's touring and weekend riders.

India sold about 11 lakh motorcycles above 250 cc in FY25, and premium bikes are the fastest-growing part of the market. Yet riders still run their passion on tools never built for them. Trails live in reels and DMs, group rides are coordinated over chat at 80 km/h, fuel and service sit in notes, and on long stretches without signal nobody can see where a rider is.

Myto Moto brings this into one place. Riders find verified trails with access status and evidence, plan routes and carry offline maps. They record every ride with a journal, fuel log and costs, even with no signal. In a group, everyone sees a live map, each rider's status, quick signals and one-tap SOS, and a check-in link lets family know when to expect them home. Fix-it guides help at the roadside, and a curated Shop is in build.

The product works today as a full test build, made by a solo founder on a free-tier stack, so running costs stay near zero until revenue exists. The next step is the App Store and Play Store release, 50 to 100 founding riders and club road captains in Bengaluru, and the first paid group ride.

The core stays free for every rider. Revenue comes from those who get the most value: organisers pay for large group rides and club tools, riders buy curated and own-brand gear, and brands sponsor labelled challenges. Safety and trail data are never for sale.

## Q02. What problem are you solving, and for whom?

For India's touring and weekend motorcyclists, and the ride leaders and clubs who organise them, every ride is pieced together across apps never built for riders. Trails hide in reels and DMs, with no way to tell if a route is open, legal or rideable after rain. Groups coordinate over chat and calls at speed, and the lead cannot see who dropped back. Off the grid, on the ghats or in Ladakh, nobody can see the rider, and a fall in a dead zone is found at the next stop.

## Q03. Why is it important to solve this problem?

More Indians are riding further every year. Bikes above 150 cc rose from 14% to 19% of motorcycle sales between FY19 and FY25 (Crisil), and Ladakh alone drew 6.1 lakh domestic tourists in 2024. These riders travel through long stretches with no signal, often in groups, often on roads and trails they do not know. Today their safety depends on luck and a chat group. A rider who falls out of sight is missed late, and riders with no trusted access information end up on closed or private land. A tool that keeps groups together, keeps people at home informed and points riders to open trails makes the sport safer as it grows.

## Q06. Your product and how it solves the problem

Myto Moto is one app for the whole ride, built offline-first so it keeps working where the signal ends.

- **Explore.** Verified trails with access status, condition and evidence, GPX import, route planning and offline maps. Riders know a trail is open and rideable before they leave. (Built)
- **Ride.** Ride recording with a journal, fuel log and costs, service reminders and a yearly recap, all working without signal. (Built)
- **Ride together.** A live group map, each rider's status, quick signals and one-tap SOS, plus clubs. The lead sees who dropped back; nobody calls at speed. A check-in link tells family the expected return and, if the rider is late, where they were last seen. (Built)
- **Equip.** Fix-it guides for roadside problems today; a curated Shop of rider gear next. (Fix-it built, Shop in build)

It works today as a full test build. The store release is the first milestone.

## Q07. What is innovative or different?

**Short version (about 150 words).** Every rider app solves one slice: maps navigate, chat coordinates, trackers record, social apps post. Myto Moto connects the whole ride, from finding a trail to getting the group home, in one app built for Indian roads. Four things set it apart. Every trail carries access status and evidence, checked before it is published, so riders know a route is open and legal. It is offline-first: maps, ride records and group status keep working where the signal ends, which is where riders most need them. Safety is built in and free for every rider: live group map, rider status, SOS and a check-in link for family. And it chooses trust over a feed: curated trails, no leaderboards that reward speed, and safety and trail data never sold. A solo founder built it on a free-tier stack, so it can stay free for riders.

**Long version.** Riders today stitch together Google Maps for navigation, WhatsApp for the group, spreadsheets for fuel, and Instagram for finding trails. Specialist apps each improve one part of this: Indian group-ride apps add a live map and clubs, global trackers add ride recording and twisty routes (usually with offline maps behind a paywall), and rider social apps add a feed and a marketplace. None connects the trail, the ride, the group and the gear, and none tells a rider whether a trail is actually open.

Myto Moto is different in four ways.

1. **Verified trails.** Each published trail carries access status, condition and evidence, checked through a moderation flow before it goes live. A pin on a map cannot tell a rider whether a route is legal or rideable after rain; Myto Moto can.
2. **Offline first.** Maps, ride recording, the fuel log and group status are built to work without signal and sync later. Indian touring happens in ghats, forests and high passes where coverage ends.
3. **Safety built in, free.** A live group map, rider status, quick signals and one-tap SOS are part of the free core, not a paid add-on. A check-in link tells family the expected return and the last-seen place if the rider is late. The family needs no app.
4. **Trust over a feed.** No speed leaderboards, no rankings, no selling of safety or trail data. Revenue comes from organisers, gear and labelled partner challenges, so the incentive is to keep riders safe and coming back, not to keep them scrolling.

The product is a full working test build, made by one founder on a free-tier stack (Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare R2), so it costs almost nothing to run until revenue exists.

## Q09. Who benefits, and the realised or intended impact

Impact below is intended, not yet measured; we start measuring at the store release.

- **Riders** get safer, easier rides: trusted trails, offline ride records and SOS, in a core that stays free, so cost is never the reason a rider rides without it.
- **Ride leaders and clubs** keep groups together: a live map of the whole group and one-tap signals in place of phone calls at speed.
- **Families at home** get peace of mind: a check-in link shows the expected return and, if the rider is late, where they were last seen. They need no app.
- **Local communities and trails** benefit when riders keep to open, legal routes instead of closed or private land.

First measures: group rides with safety features on, check-ins sent, SOS used, trails checked and published.

## Q11. Business model

**Short version.** Riders ride free. Organisers, gear buyers and partners pay: a ₹99 Large Ride Pass per group ride, a ₹279 monthly Myto Organiser plan for clubs, own-brand and curated gear, and labelled brand challenges. Safety and trail data are never for sale.

**Long version.** A free core builds the network; revenue comes from those who get the most value. All prices and margins are base-case assumptions.

- **Large Ride Pass:** ₹99 a ride, paid by the organiser, for group rides of 6 to 20 riders. Participants never pay.
- **Myto Organiser:** ₹279 a month for clubs and ride leaders who run rides often.
- **Myto Originals:** own-brand gear at about 40% gross margin.
- **Curated Shop:** about 10% commission on partner gear, sold outside the app.
- **Sponsored challenges:** brands pay ₹50,000 per clearly labelled challenge (to be validated).

The base case reaches 6,000 active riders by October 2027. Running costs stay near zero on a free-tier stack, so early revenue goes to growth rather than infrastructure.

## Q12. Social and environmental impact (for environment-themed grants)

All intended, not measured. No emissions saving is claimed.

- **Road safety:** SOS, rider status and a live group map for every rider, free, even with no signal, plus a check-in link that tells family when a rider is overdue.
- **Trail care:** only trails with checked access are published, which keeps riders off closed land and fragile ground and supports good relations with local communities.
- **Fuel you can see:** a fuel log shows each rider what their bike really burns per kilometre. Knowing it is the first step to riding more efficiently. We will report fuel logged per km once riders use the store build.
