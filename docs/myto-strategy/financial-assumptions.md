# Financial assumptions

Written 2026-11-12. **These are scenarios, not forecasts.** Myto has no store release, no billing, and no measured rider count, so there is no traction to extrapolate. Every input below is an assumption chosen to be plausible and conservative, labelled as such. No market size, competitor revenue or industry benchmark is used: none was sourced, so none is claimed. Re-run `node docs/myto-strategy/model.mjs` after changing any input; the deck imports the same model.

Money is in Indian rupees. Months run Nov 2026 (m1) to Oct 2027 (m12).

## 1. Definitions (kept separate)

| Term | Meaning | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| **GMV** | What customers pay, incl. GST: digital billings plus the whole Shop basket | Myto's income. On curated products Myto earns only a commission |
| **Revenue** | What Myto recognises, ex-GST: digital sales, Originals sales, curated commission, partnership fees | GMV; partnership fees are billed to partners and are revenue but not GMV |
| **Gross profit** | Revenue minus COGS: store fees on digital, product cost of Originals, partnership delivery cost | Contribution |
| **Contribution** | Gross profit minus other variable costs: payment gateway, fulfilment, returns | Net |
| **Opex** | Fixed monthly cost: infrastructure, developer accounts, content and illustration, community programme, legal and accounting, marketing | Founder and team pay, which is **excluded** |
| **Net** | Contribution minus opex, pre-tax and pre-founder-pay | Profit after pay |

## 2. Formulas

Per month m, per scenario (symbols are the input names in `model.mjs`):

```
MAR(m)          = PRE_LAUNCH_MAR                                   for m < launchMonth
                = PRE_LAUNCH_MAR + (marTarget - PRE_LAUNCH_MAR) * (m - launchMonth + 1) / (12 - launchMonth + 1)
organisers      = MAR * pOrganise
subscribers     = organisers * subShare                            (only once billing is live)
rides           = (organisers - subscribers) * RIDES_PER_ORGANISER
passSales       = rides * shareLarge * passConv                    (only once billing is live)
digitalGMV      = passSales * LARGE_PASS_PRICE + subscribers * ORGANISER_PRICE
digitalRev      = digitalGMV / (1 + GST_RATE)
storeFees       = digitalRev * STORE_FEE                           (COGS)

orders          = MAR * shopCtr * shopConv                         (from shopMonth)
shopGMV         = orders * aov
originalsRev    = orders * originalsShare * aov / (1 + GST_RATE)
originalsCOGS   = originalsRev * (1 - originalsGM)
curatedComm     = orders * (1 - originalsShare) * aov / (1 + GST_RATE) * curatedCommission
gateway         = orders * originalsShare * aov * SHOP_GATEWAY_FEE
fulfilment      = orders * originalsShare * SHOP_FULFIL_PER_ORDER
returns         = originalsRev * SHOP_RETURN_RATE

partnerRev      = partnerFee in each month listed in partnerMonths
partnerCOGS     = partnerRev * PARTNER_DELIVERY_COST

GMV             = digitalGMV + shopGMV
Revenue         = digitalRev + originalsRev + curatedComm + partnerRev
Gross profit    = Revenue - (storeFees + originalsCOGS + partnerCOGS)
Contribution    = Gross profit - (gateway + fulfilment + returns)
Net             = Contribution - Opex
Break-even MAR  = Opex(m12) / (product contribution per active rider at m12)   (partnerships excluded)
```

Advertising and event ticketing are **not modelled** (zero): advertising is deferred and ticketing is a hypothesis with no basis for a number.

## 4. How to read the result

- **No scenario earns a year-one profit.** Net is negative in all three; the base case ends the year about ₹3.2 lakh down on these opex assumptions, with a peak cumulative loss near ₹3.3 lakh in base and ambitious. This is before any founder or team pay.
- **Revenue is small in rupees.** At 6,000 active riders (base), about 87 Large Ride Passes and 18 organiser subscriptions a month at Oct 27 produce roughly ₹11,600 a month of digital revenue. The Shop adds about 9 orders a month.
- **Partnerships dominate the base case**: ₹1,00,000 of year-one revenue is two sponsored challenges, which is a hypothesis with no evidence behind it. Without them the base year-one revenue is about ₹86,000.
- **In the base case GMV (₹1.33 lakh) is lower than revenue (₹1.86 lakh)** only because partnership fees are revenue but not GMV. They are not contradictory.
- **Break-even needs far more riders than the targets**: product-only break-even is about 17,500 to 30,000 active riders at these conversion rates, against targets of 1,500, 6,000 and 20,000. Opex scales up with the scenario here by design (more content and marketing), so this is not a statement about a fixed cost base; it is a statement that the conversion assumptions are low and the product is not a cash business in year one.
- **Implication**: treat year one as validation on a free-tier, founder-funded cost base. The levers that move the answer, in order, are active riders, the share of organisers who pay, Shop order value and margin, and cutting opex. Each is testable by the end of Phase 3.

## 5. What would make these numbers real

Replace each assumption with a measurement as it becomes available (roadmap C1): MAR from consented analytics, organiser share and pass conversion from the first months of billing, Shop clicks and orders from attributed links, margin from actual supplier and fulfilment quotes. Until then do not quote any figure here outside this document and the deck without the word "assumption".
